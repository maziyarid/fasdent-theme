# Privacy Policy Production Checklist

این فایل متن حقوقی نهایی نیست؛ Gate محتوای Privacy است. قبل Production، Privacy Page واقعی باید با implementation و vendor inventory تطابق داده و توسط Site Owner/Legal تأیید شود.

- Contact form: fields, purpose, retention, recipients.
- Appointment request: fields, purpose, confirmation-by-clinic semantics, retention.
- SMTP/provider logs and retention.
- Hosting/server logs and retention.
- Analytics / consent layer, if enabled.
- Microsoft Clarity or any session/behavior tool, if enabled.
- Maps/social/third-party embeds or outbound services actually used.
- Data-subject/contact channel and policy update date.
- No vendor may be named unless it is actually enabled in the production inventory.
